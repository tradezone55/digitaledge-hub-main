About DigitalEdge Learning Systems

Interactive certification training built by an active networking instructor.

Our Mission Why DigitalEdge exists

DigitalEdge was created to solve a simple problem: students need quality, accessible practice tools to succeed in their IT certifications and technical courses. As an instructor in the school and college system, I saw students struggling to find affordable, effective exam simulators and study resources. So I built DigitalEdge.

Our commitment: Free Practice will always be free. No login, no paywall, no trial clock — the exam sims, quizzes, and study tools stay open to everyone. Tutor Command and Premier Pro are paid systems, and they're what keep the free tier funded and maintained.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is DigitalEdge really free?

A: Free Practice is — completely. No login, no paywall, no trial clock. Tutor Command and Premier Pro are paid systems, and they're what keep the free tier funded and maintained.

Q: Who can use DigitalEdge?

A: Anyone preparing for IT certifications — students, career changers, professionals, or anyone looking to expand their technical knowledge.

Q: How is this funded?

A: Tutor Command and Premier Pro subscriptions fund the free tier, alongside supporters — organizations, schools, and companies who want to keep quality education accessible.

Q: Can instructors use this in their classrooms?

A: Absolutely! That's exactly what it's built for. Feel free to integrate these tools into your curriculum and share with your students.

Q: How can my organization support DigitalEdge?

A: Contact us about sponsorship opportunities. We offer recognition for supporters and can discuss custom needs for your organization.

Q: Are the practice exams updated regularly?

A: Yes! We continuously update our question banks and content to reflect the latest certification exam objectives and industry standards.
